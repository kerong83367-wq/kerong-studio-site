How to play Snake

Use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard, swipe on a touch screen, or tap the on-screen arrows on mobile. Each apple you eat makes the snake one segment longer and adds 10 points. The snake speeds up as it grows, so plan your turns early and use the open space in the middle of the board while you still have it.

Tips

  • Stick to the edges when the snake gets long — it keeps escape routes open.
  • Never turn back on yourself: a 180-degree turn ends the game instantly.
  • Short, safe detours beat risky dives across the whole board.
