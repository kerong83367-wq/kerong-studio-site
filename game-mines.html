How to play Minesweeper

Click a square to reveal it. A number tells you how many mines touch that square (including diagonals). An empty square opens its whole neighborhood automatically. Right-click a square (or switch to Flag mode and tap) to plant a flag where you believe a mine is hiding. Your first click is always safe. You win when every safe square is open; one wrong click on a mine ends the game.

Tips

  • A "1" in a corner with only one covered neighbor means that neighbor is a mine.
  • Flag as you deduce — flags protect squares from accidental clicks.
  • When a number already touches enough flags, its other neighbors are safe to open.
