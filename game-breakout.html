How to play Breakout

Move the paddle with your mouse, by touching and dragging, or with the left and right arrow keys. Click or press Space to launch the ball. Every brick you break scores points; where the ball hits the paddle controls its angle — edge hits send it off at a sharp angle, center hits send it straighter up. You have three lives per game. Clear all the bricks to reach the next level, where the ball moves faster.

Tips

  • Open a tunnel to the top early — a ball bouncing above the wall racks up points fast.
  • Stay under the ball's shadow instead of chasing it at the last second.
  • Use paddle edges for sharp angles when you need to reach side bricks.
