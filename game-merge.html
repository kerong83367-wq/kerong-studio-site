How to play Number Merge

Press the arrow keys (or WASD), swipe on a touch screen, or use the on-screen arrows. All tiles slide as far as they can in that direction. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one tile worth double. After every move a new 2 or 4 tile appears in a random empty spot. The game ends when the board is full and no merges are possible — so keep your biggest tile tucked in a corner and build around it.

Tips

  • Pick one corner for your biggest tile and never move it away from there.
  • Keep your tiles sorted in descending order along one edge, like a snake.
  • Avoid the direction opposite your build — it drags your big tiles into the open.
