How to play Memory Match

Click or tap any card to flip it over, then flip a second one. If the two pictures match, the pair stays face up. If not, both cards turn back over — try to remember where they were. One "move" is one pair of flips. Finish all 8 pairs to win, and challenge yourself to do it in fewer moves each time.

Tips

  • Work through the board in a fixed order so you build a mental map.
  • Say the positions out loud ("rocket, top row, second") — it genuinely helps.
  • Early flips are investments: every card you have seen makes the endgame faster.
